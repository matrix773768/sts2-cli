# F3B experiment contract

- Frozen F3A roots, in the original manifest order, with immutable local native checkpoints. All five are development roots from a shared Act 1 lineage.
- A uses F2 Q1 card scoring and its supported Headbutt selection, without potions or search. B uses width-8 breadth-layered beam and a fixed rank from public progress, HP, block, energy and enemy count. C and D use the same ordered four initial strategies. C proposes repairs from death evidence. D uses fixed early/middle anchors independently of the cause. Both use FIFO, with a single-action substitution, three-decision same-turn window, strategic suffix rebuild and undirected edit.
- B/C/D test independently with potions disallowed and allowed. Each group receives the same native-compatible card/target/EndTurn menu and the same allowlist when enabled. One F2-ranked Headbutt choice per card/target is used in every arm. No teacher actions enter the search.
- Cumulative Replay ceilings 128, 512 and 2,048. One deterministic 2,048-transition run per cell measures its prefixes; earlier ceilings never borrow later results. 2,048 was chosen below the smallest F3A teacher transition budget actually consumed and to bound full-trajectory logging. Maximum route length 48, hot search wall ceiling 12 seconds, 256 MiB managed heap and working-set safety stop.
- Stop at a complete predicted win and verify from a separate native checkpoint process. A rejected route is blocked by its action digest; restarting consumes the remaining transition budget, including all recomputation. At most three verification attempts per cell. Record actual Replay calls, first accepted win, all complete failures, budget truncation, coverage and mismatch separately.
- Evaluate the BOWLBUGS deaths and TUNNELER wins separately. The two treatments may change the trajectories; potion-enabled success alone does not identify a necessary potion. Original F3A costs are a descriptive benchmark measured in a separate run.
